# Master CV Claim Audit

Every substantive CV claim should be tracked here before being treated as authoritative.

## Status values
- VERIFIED: supported by records, source material, or direct confirmation
- NEEDS_SOURCE: plausible but needs supporting evidence
- NEEDS_QUANTIFICATION: experience is verified but metric/scale needs confirmation
- REMOVE: inaccurate, unsupported, or inappropriate for the master CV
